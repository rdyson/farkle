    How to Play

    The first listed player starts. Play follows the entered order. Start every turn with six dice. After each roll, set aside at least one scoring die or combination. Then bank the turn’s points or reroll the remaining dice.

    A Farkle ends the turn and loses only its unbanked points. Combinations must come from one roll; they cannot be built across rolls.

    Hot dice: Opening score:

    The first player to reach the selected winning threshold (10,000 points) starts the final round. Every other player gets one last turn in the existing order. A challenger must finish strictly higher than the current score to beat; a tie leaves the existing leader in front.

    Enter each banked turn score yourself. The scorekeeper adds it to the player’s total but does not check the dice.
