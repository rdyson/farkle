    How to Play

    Roll the physical dice and enter the points banked for that turn. Enter zero when a player Farkles. The app follows the shown turn order and adds each score to the running total.

    The first player to reach the winning score—10,000 points by default—starts the final round. Every other player gets one last turn. A challenger must beat the score to take the lead; a tie is not enough.
